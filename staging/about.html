Who is Rory?

Hi, I'm Rory. I am a father, a husband, a tech enthusiast. I am currently pursuing a degree in Information Networking and Telecommunications (INT) with a focus on Fullstack Development at Fort Hays State University.

My interest in tech started when I was a kid, but it wasn't until 2020 that I treating it as a serious career path. In 2014, I earned my general education credits at Neosho County Community College along with an Associate's degree in Fine Arts. In between then and 2025, I fostered a passion for tech and transferred to Fort Hays State University to study Information Networking and Telecommunications with an emphasis on Fullstack Development.

Despite being early in my academic and professional career, I am a driven, adaptable and determined individual committed to producing work that reflects the depth of my curiosity and standard I hold myself to. I learn quickly, take feedback well, and approach every challenge like a problem waiting to be solved.
